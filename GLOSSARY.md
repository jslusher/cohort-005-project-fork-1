# Course Platform

An online course platform where Students buy courses and work through their lessons.

## People

**Student**:
A person who buys courses and takes their lessons.
_Avoid_: Learner

**Admin**:
A person who runs the platform.

## Buying

**Purchase**:
A Student buying a course.

**Enrollment**:
A Student's access to a course's content, given by a Purchase or a redeemed Seat.

**Team Purchase**:
A Purchase of several Seats in one course, for handing out to others.

**Seat**:
One place in a course from a Team Purchase, handed out as a link that someone redeems.
_Avoid_: Coupon, claim

## Cancellations

**Cancellation**:
A Student's request to undo a whole Purchase and get their money back, which an Admin approves or declines.
_Avoid_: Refund, chargeback, reversal

**Cancellation window**:
The 14 days after a Purchase during which a Student may ask for a Cancellation.
_Avoid_: Refund window

**Eligible for Cancellation**:
Describes a Purchase the Student may still ask to cancel.
_Avoid_: Cancellation offer, refundable

**Amount returned**:
What a Student gets back when a Cancellation is approved: exactly what they paid.
_Avoid_: Amount refunded
